REFUND & RETURN POLICY

Our policy for returns, refunds and order cancellations

Home / Refund & Return Policy

At AMAKART, we aim to provide quality products and a satisfactory shopping experience. Our products are prepared through a Print-on-Demand (POD) fulfillment process, where items are printed and produced after an order is placed.

Please read this policy carefully before placing your order.

Important: Since our products are made to order, returns and exchanges are generally not accepted for change of mind or incorrect size selection. However, verified damaged, defective or incorrect products will be reviewed for an appropriate resolution.

1. Returns and Exchanges

AMAKART products are made to order through a Print-on-Demand process. Therefore, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges for:

  • Incorrect size selected by the customer.
  • Change of mind after placing an order.
  • Personal preference or dissatisfaction with the chosen design.
  • Minor colour variations between product images and actual products.

Please check the product description, size chart and available options carefully before placing your order.

2. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, misprinted or different from what you ordered, please contact AMAKART.

Eligible issues may include:

  • Product received with physical damage.
  • Manufacturing defects.
  • Incorrect or missing print.
  • Wrong product delivered.
  • Significant printing defects.

If the issue is verified, AMAKART may arrange a replacement or refund, depending on the circumstances.

3. How to Request a Refund or Replacement

To report a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us within 7 days of delivery.

Please provide the following information:

  • Your AMAKART Order ID.
  • Clear photographs of the received product.
  • Photographs of the packaging, where relevant.
  • An unboxing video, if available.
  • A brief description of the issue.

Our team will review your request and inform you about the next steps. Requests may be rejected if the reported issue cannot be verified.

4. Order Cancellation

Orders can only be cancelled before they enter the production or fulfillment process.

Once production has started, cancellation may not be possible because the product is being prepared specifically for your order.

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible with your order ID.

5. Wrong Size or Customer Preference

Customers are responsible for selecting the correct size, colour and product variant before checkout.

Returns or exchanges are generally not accepted for incorrectly selected sizes or changes in personal preference.

If you receive a product with a verified manufacturing defect, please contact us for review.

6. Shipping and Undelivered Orders

Please ensure that your delivery address, mobile number and PIN code are correct when placing an order.

If an order is returned due to an incorrect address, customer unavailability or refusal to accept delivery, additional shipping charges may apply for reshipping.

If a shipment is confirmed lost during transit, please contact us so that we can investigate and provide an appropriate resolution.

7. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed to the original payment method wherever possible.

  • Refund requests are subject to verification.
  • Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider or bank.
  • Customers will be informed once a refund has been initiated.

Refunds will be handled in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

8. Colour and Size Variations

Actual product colours may vary slightly from the images displayed on your screen due to lighting, display settings and printing processes.

Minor measurement differences within standard manufacturing tolerances may occur and do not automatically qualify as product defects.

9. Contact Us

For refund, return, replacement or order-related assistance, please contact our customer support team.
